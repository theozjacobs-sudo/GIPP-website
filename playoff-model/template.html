    How the model works. Monte Carlo simulations of the remaining fixtures. Each team gets an attack and defence rating from its season goals for and against relative to the league average ( goals per team per game), shrunk toward average with three games' worth of prior so a 7–1 doesn't make us look like Brazil. Scorelines are drawn from independent Poisson distributions. Everything is played at Pier 5, so there's no home advantage. The final table is scored points → goal difference → goals scored, then a coin flip; the league hasn't published its tiebreakers, and head-to-head would change very little here.
