# New Season, New Us (Same Results)

The winter 2025 Group Stage NYC season kicks off Tuesday night and, once again, Good Intent Poor Product F.C. enters the campaign full of irrational belief. Our preseason consisted of exactly one scrimmage, two group-chat arguments about formation, and a deeply optimistic spreadsheet no one has opened since.

Tuesday nights at Brooklyn Bridge Park hit different when it is 28 degrees and the wind off the East River is personally attacking your face. Half the squad will show up in base layers that cost more than our league dues. The other half will be in cotton shorts and regret.

But here is the thing — none of that matters. Every season we tell ourselves *this is the one* where we finally put it all together for a full ninety minutes. Where the passing clicks, the press holds, and the keeper does not have to make fourteen saves. History says otherwise. Five championships say we peak when it counts anyway.

So lace up, layer up, and remember the motto: *i remain transfixed by the flame of good intentions.*

See you under the lights. GIPP forever.
