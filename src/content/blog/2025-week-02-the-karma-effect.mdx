# The Karma Effect

Let the record show what happened on the night of Championship No. 5.

We had just scraped through the semifinal — one of those ugly, beautiful wins where you survive on vibes and a goalkeeping masterclass. The squad was huddled up, still breathing heavy, when a man approached. He introduced himself simply: *Karma.* From Nepal. Passing through Brooklyn. Wanted a photo with the team.

We obliged, obviously. You do not say no to a man literally named Karma. He posed, smiled, shook hands, and said: "Good luck in the final." Then he disappeared into the night like some kind of footballing spirit guide.

Reader, we won the final. Convincingly. The kind of performance where every pass found its target and every tackle stuck. Five-time champions. The GIPP dynasty rolls on.

Was it tactics? Fitness? The hours on the training pitch? Maybe. But we know the truth. A stranger named Karma blessed this club, and the universe delivered.

(Check our Instagram for the photo. It is, and we cannot stress this enough, *very real.*)
