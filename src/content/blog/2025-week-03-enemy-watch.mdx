# Enemy Watch: Scouting Report Vol. 1

**CLASSIFICATION: TOP SECRET — GIPP EYES ONLY**

The intelligence department has been operational since the schedule dropped. Here are our early findings.

## Target Alpha — "The Matching Kit Guys"

Threat level: **3/5.** They showed up week one in full matching kits with squad numbers. That level of organization is unsettling. However, our sources confirm they spent more on jerseys than on practice. Exploitable.

## Target Bravo — "The One Good Player FC"

Threat level: **4/5.** Every rec league has one. A team of average lads built around a single absolute baller who could probably play three divisions up. Game plan: foul him early, foul him often. (Legally. Mostly.)

## Target Charlie — "The Guys Who Have Been Playing Together Since Birth"

Threat level: **2/5.** Chemistry is high but they have not updated their tactics since 2016. They play a rigid 4-4-2 and rely on long balls. We eat long balls for breakfast.

## Summary

The league is competitive but nothing we have not seen before. GIPP has the intangibles. We have the spreadsheets. We have the post-game debriefs.

They do not know what is coming. Honestly, neither do we. That is our greatest weapon.

*End transmission.*
